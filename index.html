About

Ifeoluwa is a digital marketing and social media professional with practical experience in social media management, content strategy, content planning, and performance analysis.

She specializes in creating content that feels relevant to the audience while still serving a clear purpose for the brand — developing content ideas and calendars, producing short-form videos and social media content, writing captions, managing content schedules, and using insights and KPIs to understand what's working.

She's worked within a marketing agency, collaborating with a team to support social media activities and content strategies for brands — and she's especially drawn to the space where creativity meets strategy: understanding an audience, turning insights into ideas, and creating content that drives engagement and visibility.
